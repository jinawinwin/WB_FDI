Reading the rankings

FDI net flows can be negative when disinvestment exceeds new investment. This is a balance-of-payments flow measure, not the size of accumulated foreign investment assets or liabilities.

Panel coverage

The panel retains missing observations as nulls. It does not treat missing reports as zero. Global totals use the World Bank's published WLD aggregate; group totals sum available country observations.

A country’s historical group assignment uses its currently reported World Bank income and regional classification. For historical classification-sensitive research, classification-vintage controls should be added in a later phase.

06 · BILATERAL
Important data boundary: the World Bank WDI indicators in the first two tabs are country-level annual net flows and do not identify investor or destination countries. The bilateral layer must use a partner-level source. IMF's Direct Investment Positions by Counterpart Economy (DIP, formerly CDIS) provides inward and outward positions by immediate counterpart economy. These are FDI stocks/positions, not annual WDI flows.

Source and integration plan

The repository includes a schema template at data/bilateral_fdi_positions.csv. No bilateral observations are invented or inferred from aggregate flows.

Expected fields include reporting economy, counterpart economy, reference year, direction, instrument (equity/debt), position value, reported vs mirror-data flag, source and source URL. This allows observed bilateral positions and mirrored counterpart estimates to be distinguished.
