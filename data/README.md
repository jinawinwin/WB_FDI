# Dataset directory

Files generated by `scripts/update_fdi_data.py`:

- `fdi_country_panel.csv/.json`: one row per reporting economy and year; country identifiers, current World Bank classifications, continent, coordinates, FDI flows and GDP/population.
- `fdi_global_panel.csv/.json`: World Bank WLD aggregate observations, fetched directly rather than calculated by summing country rows.
- `fdi_group_panel.csv/.json`: totals by current World Bank income classification, World Bank region, continent, and analyst-defined overlapping strategy cluster.
- `data_status.json`: refresh time, sample coverage, latest non-missing year by indicator, sources and interpretation notes.
- `bilateral_fdi_positions.csv`: schema only until validated IMF DIP data are integrated.

All FDI amounts in the WDI panel are annual **net flows** in current US dollars unless the field indicates percentage of GDP. The bilateral schema is for FDI **positions/stocks**, and must not be combined with flow series as if they were the same measure.

Missing data remain missing. The values for group totals are sums of the available reporting economies. Group percentages of GDP are calculated as aggregate FDI flow divided by aggregate GDP, not as an unweighted average of country percentages.

The strategic clusters are transparent analyst-defined lists in `config/country_clusters.csv`. They overlap and are not official classifications.
